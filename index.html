Undergraduate Thesis · Jul 2025 – Jul 2026

Dual-Band Metasurface Wearable Antenna for Pilot Health Monitoring

Supervisor: Md. Abu Bakar Siddik · Co-authors: Md Rashid Karim, Md. Sakib Hossain Porosh
2.45 GHz ISM5.8 GHz ISMFR-4 · 30 × 14 × 1.6 mm³3 × 3 AMC
  • BAF emblem-shaped dual-band microstrip patch antenna for on-body sensing (2.45 GHz) and an off-body cockpit link (5.8 GHz).
  • Four-stage geometry evolution and parametric studies of the partial ground plane in CST Studio Suite.
  • Dual-band coplanar AMC unit cell arranged as a 3 × 3 array behind the antenna, with a 3.25 mm foam spacer.
  • On-body evaluation on a four-layer phantom (denim, skin, fat, muscle); equivalent-circuit model in Keysight ADS.
  • Simulated: realized gain 4.52 / 6.93 dBi; peak 10 g SAR reduced from 10.93 / 15.30 W/kg to 0.972 / 1.609 W/kg (2.45 / 5.8 GHz), below the ICNIRP 2 W/kg limit.
  • Simulation study; fabrication and measurement are future work. Manuscript in preparation for PIER.
IEEE MARSCON 2026 · Paper ID 344

ML-Assisted Frequency-Reconfigurable Trophy-Shaped Microstrip Antenna

UWB ~3.9–11.5 GHz2 PIN diodes · 3 modesRT/duroid 5880 · 0.127 mm
  • Compact CPW-fed trophy-shaped antenna on Rogers RT/duroid 5880 (εr ≈ 2.2), 47 × 25 mm.
  • Three modes: UWB (~3.9–11.5 GHz), tri-band (~4.3 / 7.6 / 10.5 GHz) and asymmetric (~4.15 / 7.8 / 10.3 GHz).
  • Linear Regression, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting and Stacking surrogates trained on CST data (9 geometric inputs → 2 resonances + S11 depths).
  • Random Forest selected: R² = 0.986 / 0.997 (resonant frequencies) and 0.963 / 0.974 (S11 depths) on held-out CST samples.
  • Reported in the paper (simulated): radiation efficiency above 90%; gain up to about 4 dB in tri-band mode.
